How it works

From email to pipeline, in five steps.

One product. One job. Catch every sales signal in your team's inbox and turn it into clean, qualified pipeline — written to Salesforce, HubSpot, or Zoho with an audit trail on every action.

01

An email lands.

A customer asks for a demo. A prospect sends an RFP. A renewal contact replies with a question. ZUUZ reads every inbound message in your sales mailbox the moment it arrives.

02

Signals get extracted.

Source, intent, urgency, industry, budget, who's asking, what they want. Every signal in the message is classified and surfaced — no rep has to type anything in.

03

The lead gets qualified.

As the conversation progresses, cold leads warm up. By the third or fourth exchange, ZUUZ has the full requirement assembled — what they're buying, when, why, and what's blocking them.

04

The next action is drafted.

Reply suggested. Follow-up sequence proposed. Meeting times pulled from the rep's calendar. For RFPs, your product catalog is matched against the customer's requirement — gaps and risks flagged before the proposal goes out.

05

Pipeline is captured.

Quotes sent, discounts given, deals closed, renewals coming up, upsell signals — every line of pipeline lives in the CRM, in real time, with the source email attached. The sales report writes itself.
