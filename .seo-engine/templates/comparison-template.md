# Comparison Template — ZUUZ vs {Competitor}

> Use this for all `ZUUZ vs X` comparison posts. Lead with the competitor's
> strengths — readers can smell trash-talking and discount the whole post.

---

## Frontmatter

```yaml
title: "ZUUZ vs {Competitor}: {Decision Frame}"   # ≤ 60 chars
description: "{What this comparison answers}"      # ≤ 160 chars
date: "{YYYY-MM-DD}"
```

## Structure

### 1. Hook (≤ 150 words)

State the decision the reader is actually trying to make. Not "let's compare two
tools" — something like "you've shortlisted {Competitor} and ZUUZ — here's the
real fork in the road."

### 2. TL;DR table

| Axis | ZUUZ | {Competitor} |
|------|------|--------------|
| Built for | sales inbox → CRM | {their category} |
| Time to value | days | {x} |
| RFP catalog matching | ✅ (10:1 at Nesto) | {verify} |
| Bi-directional CRM sync | Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho | {verify} |
| Audit trail per AI action | ✅ | {verify} |
| Human approval gates | ✅ default-on | {verify} |
| Pricing | from $1,500/mo | {verify} |
| Best for | inbound-heavy B2B sales | {verify} |

> Every "{verify}" row must be checked against the competitor's live site on
> the publish date. Set `last_verified` in competitors.yaml.

### 3. Where {Competitor} is strongest

3–5 specifics. Be generous. Cite their site or a credible third party.

### 4. Where ZUUZ is strongest

3–5 specifics. Back each with a customer example or metric:

- **Customer example** — RA Technologies / Cloud Box Technologies / Nesto
- **Metric** — $120K pipeline / 10:1 SKU matching / 60-day payback

### 5. Use cases each is best for

Two side-by-side blocks. Pretend you're advising a friend.

### 6. Pricing comparison

Honest table. Note tier overlaps.

### 7. Decision framework

A short flow: "If your team is primarily {X}, pick {Y}." Don't end with "of
course you should pick ZUUZ."

### 8. FAQ (from People Also Ask data)

Build this from real PAA questions for "{Competitor} vs ZUUZ" or
"{Competitor} alternative". Don't invent questions.

### 9. Soft CTA

One paragraph. No hard sell. Link to demo or related cluster pages.

- [ ] At least one concrete ZUUZ metric cited
- [ ] All competitor claims verified against their live site (note date)
- [ ] Author bio (Avinash Gujje) at end with link
- [ ] Internal link to `/blog/ai-sales-agents-buyers-guide` (cluster pillar)
- [ ] Internal link to at least one other comparison page
- [ ] FAQ drawn from real PAA, not invented

## What this template explicitly forbids

- Trash-talking the competitor
- Unverified or stale (90+ days) competitor claims
- Generic "ZUUZ is just better" assertions
- Stuffing the comparison with axes where ZUUZ is clearly weaker (Enterprise scale, ecosystem depth, etc.) — list them honestly, don't hide them
